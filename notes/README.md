# Notes

Continuous, under every phase: a decision that lives only in a chat window did
not happen.

| Folder | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| `meetings/` | Meeting notes and transcripts, `YYYY-MM-DD-<topic>.md`. Decisions and action items pulled out at the top. |
| `conversations/` | Emails, messages and calls that are not meetings, same naming and rules. |
| `tips/` | Profiles of the people involved: role, what they care about, how they decide, what to avoid. INTERNAL. |
| `client-files/` | Documents the client gave us that we work from (specs, packets, drawings), filed from `inbox/`. Never edited; a derived copy says what it came from. |

Recorded conversations are reviewed before they are trusted: a transcript can
mishear a number. Anything load-bearing gets confirmed and lands in
`client.md` (scope) or `protoboard/docs/decisions.md` (design).
