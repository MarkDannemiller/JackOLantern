# Research

Phase 1, then deep storage: what we read and learned before and while
deciding. `links.md` is the ledger of sources, `questions.md` the open and
answered questions. Add `papers/`, `concepts/` or `images/` when there is
something to put in them, not before.

Part datasheets and the evidence behind a part choice do not go here: they
belong to the part, in the stack project (`protoboard/parts/evidence/`).
